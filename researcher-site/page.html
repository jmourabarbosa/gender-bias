About

I study how humans reason logically before they have language. My work asks which elementary logical operations, such as disjunction and the elimination of alternatives, are already available to preverbal infants, and how these processes show up in behaviour and in the brain.

To answer this I combine eye-tracking, pupillometry and neuroimaging with experimental paradigms designed for infants and adults. I completed my PhD at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona, with a thesis on the behavioural and neural correlates of logical inferences, and I am now a postdoctoral researcher at NeuroSpin.
