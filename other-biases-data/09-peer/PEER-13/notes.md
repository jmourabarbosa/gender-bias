## Headline number
MCF qualification rate (qualifications / dossiers examined) 64.8% (8,045 / 12,416); PR 59.4% (293 / 493). By discipline group (MCF): from 33.8% (Droit et science politique) to 84.5% (Sciences de la terre) (PDF p.3 text and figure 3). 74.9% of MCF candidates obtained at least one qualification (p.2).
