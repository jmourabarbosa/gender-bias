(2022), "Rethinking the funding line at the Swiss National Science Foundation: Bayesian ranking and lottery", Statistics and Public Policy 9(1):110-121 (arXiv:2102.09958).

- **access_status:** downloaded

## Headline number
News page: after the Postdoc.Mobility pilot, lots may be drawn in all schemes from 2021; "Only very few applications will be subject to the drawing of lots" - no percentage given. Paper (PDF p.8 Table 1; p.9-10 text, Fig. 2): Feb. 2020 junior fellowship call, 181 submissions, 79 discussed; 8 proposals (Medicine 4, STEM 4) were put in the random-selection group, 4 funded by lot = 4.4% of submissions (10% of discussed proposals) in the draw zone.

**Key-number check:** **NOT VERIFIED / possible MISMATCH** - "~3% of proposals affected" is not stated on the SNSF page or in the paper; the documented pilot case gives 8/181 = 4.4% of submissions in the draw zone (2.2% decided by lot).
