## Headline number
DORA page: Experiment! (2013-2020/21) received >5,000 applications for 183 grants; lottery cohort showed "more early career grantees, more women ... more minority subject areas", and jury-selected vs lottery-selected cohorts had "very similar research outputs and outcomes". Interim report (PDF p.1-2): since 2017 about half of projects drawn by lot; grants per year roughly doubled; survey (n = 25, Tab. 2 PDF p.3): 92% see more individual equal opportunity, 88% avoidance of bias, 56% fear lower quality.

**Key-number check:** **MATCH** (qualitative: same output, more diverse winners). No quantitative cohort comparison table is public in what was fetched.
