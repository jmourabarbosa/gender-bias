## Headline number
"50% of the variation in reviewer quality scores was subjective in origin" (abstract, PDF p.1). Committees agreed on about half of accepted papers: Committee 2 accepted 22 of Committee 1's 43 accepts (51%) (PDF p.3, Table 1).
