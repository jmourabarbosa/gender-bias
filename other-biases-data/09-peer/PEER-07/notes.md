# PEER-07 - Mahoney 1977, confirmatory bias in the peer review system

- **Citation:** Mahoney M.J. (1977), "Publication Prejudices: An Experimental Study of Confirmatory Bias in the Peer Review System", Cognitive Therapy and Research 1(2):161-175. DOI 10.1007/BF01173636.
- **url_used:** https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~wstarbuc/Writing/Prejud.htm (page.html, first pass; full-text web transcription on W. Starbuck's NYU page)
- **access_status:** downloaded (web transcription of the full article; no PDF pages - "page" refers to the HTML)

## Headline number
75 reviewers of a behaviour-modification journal, 67 reviews returned; identical manuscripts differing only in results. Mean summary recommendation (0 = reject ... 6 = accept): positive results 3.2, negative 1.8, no results 3.4, mixed 0.5 / 1.4; methodology rated 4.2 (positive) vs 2.4 (negative) (Table I in page.html). Inter-rater agreement on relevance -0.07.

**Key-number check:** **MATCH** (confirming results rated higher).
