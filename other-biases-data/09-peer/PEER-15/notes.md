## Headline number
DORA profile: Inserm signed DORA in 2018; the 2021 external (Hcéres) evaluation reports that "during evaluation there is still a strong dependence on traditional metrics except for researchers in the new grade 'classe exceptionnelle'" and that "external agencies (e.g. funders) still evaluate applicants based on traditional metrics" (report p.18, quoted). Criteria PDF p.1: "L'Inserm est signataire de la Déclaration de San Francisco (DORA), veuillez ne pas utiliser l'Impact Factor (IF) comme mesure de substitution de la qualité des articles", but juries still assess signature rank "cohérent avec le grade de DR2 (dernière position ...)".
