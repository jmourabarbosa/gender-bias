# LOC-08 - Horta, Veloso & Grediaga (2010), Navel gazing: academic inbreeding and scientific productivity

## Headline number
Inbred faculty publish 15% fewer peer-reviewed articles than non-inbred (marginal effect -0.41 on a mean of 2.82, p<0.05) but have 46% more consulting contracts (Table 3, PDF p.17); ~40% less likely to exchange information with external colleagues (Table 4 p.16). Data: Mexico (incl. UNAM), 1999-2002.

**Key-number check:** **MATCH** (inbred faculty publish less: -15%).
