## Headline number
42% of FRS/FBA Fellows with known UK schooling attended independent schools (Royal Society 38%, British Academy 47%) vs 7% of pupils (Table 1, PDF p.6; p.3). 56% studied at Oxbridge (p.2, Table 4 p.8). Only 2% from non-selective state schools.

**Key-number check:** Minor MISMATCH: key number says 40% (THE's rounding); the report says 42% (Table 1 p.6). 7% matches.
