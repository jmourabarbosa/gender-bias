## Headline number
2016-17: students from very advantaged backgrounds = 64% of grandes écoles students vs 23% of the 20-24 age group and 47% of all bac+3-5 students (PDF p.11 and fig. 4.1 p.93; ENS 72%). Trend: 64% (2006) -> 65% (2016) in grandes écoles (constant 2006 scope), top 10% most selective 77% -> 78% (fig. 7.17 p.215).
