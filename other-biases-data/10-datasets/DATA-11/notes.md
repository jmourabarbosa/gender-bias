- **Citation:** Pölönen J., Rushforth A. (2025), "CoARA by numbers dataset", Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17846515 (companion to the preprint "CoARA by numbers: Research assessment reform uptake in European higher education", doi 10.31235/osf.io/69vjr_v1).
- **url_used:** https://zenodo.org/api/records/17846515/files/eter_coara_dataset_2022.xlsx/content
- **access_status:** downloaded

## Files
- `data/eter_coara_dataset_2022.xlsx` (3,439 European HEIs, ETER reference year 2022, with CoARA signature/membership/action plan 2025, DORA 2025, HR award, Leiden Ranking, alliances); `data/zenodo_record.json`.
- `data/DATA-11_by_country.csv`: our aggregation by country (counts and % signatories, overall and among PhD-awarding HEIs). Script `scripts/other/data11_coara.py`.

## Headline number (our computation)
Europe: 450 of 3,439 HEIs (13.1%) signed CoARA; 27.1% of PhD-awarding HEIs. France: 45 of 783 HEIs (5.7%) but 37 of 101 PhD-awarding HEIs (36.6%); 26 French HEIs signed DORA; 3 French CoARA action plans published. Spain 62%, Italy 54%, Germany 10% of PhD-awarding HEIs.
