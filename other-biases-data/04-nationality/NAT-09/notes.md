# NAT-09: Geographical diversity of peer reviewers shapes author success

- **Citation:** Zumel Dumlao J. M., Teplitskiy M. (2025). "Geographical diversity of peer reviewers shapes author success". *PNAS* 122(33): e2507394122. DOI 10.1073/pnas.2507394122. PMC12377754. Data: 60 STEM journals of IOP Publishing.
- **url_used:** PMC open-data bucket https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC12377754.1/ (first pass: source.pdf, article.xml, figure JPGs, supplementary/pnas.2507394122.sapp.pdf). page.html is the Peer Review Congress abstract.
- **Access status:** downloaded
- **Headline number found:** same-country reviewers are 4.78 percentage points more likely to review positively than other reviewers of the same manuscript (P < 0.0001). Abstract p.1 and results PDF p.3, Fig. 1. Authors from the USA, China and India have a 30.27%, 26.59% and 13.69% chance that a reviewer is from their own country, vs 2.66% for low/lower-middle-income countries other than India (p.4, Fig. 2B, SI Table S9).
- **Key number check (same-country reviewer +4.78 points):** MATCH
