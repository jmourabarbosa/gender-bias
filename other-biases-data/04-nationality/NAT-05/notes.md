# NAT-05: Passeport talent testimony (Services Publics+, Sous-préfecture de Saint-Denis)

- **Citation:** Services Publics+ (DITP), testimony "Délai 5 mois passeport talent - Sous-préfecture de Saint-Denis", experience n°7327070, posted about April 2026, with the prefecture's reply. Anonymous user. Licence etalab-2.0.
- **url_used:** https://www.plus.transformation.gouv.fr/experiences/7327070_delai-5-mois-passeport-talent-sous-prefecture-de-saint-denis (first pass, page.html, checked as genuine)
- **Access status:** downloaded (web page)
- **Headline content:** a public-health master's graduate hired by a cancer research institute (start date 5 Jan 2026) filed for a "Passeport Talent - Chercheur" on 14 Nov 2025. Five months later the file was still "en cours d'instruction". With no API (attestation de prolongation d'instruction), the employment contract could not be signed. The previous permit expired at the end of November 2025, and CAF and housing benefits were suspended. Five other users reported a similar experience. The prefecture gave a generic reply.
- **Key number check (~5-month permit wait):** MATCH (anecdotal, one testimony; not a statistic)
- **Caveat:** single self-reported case, Seine-Saint-Denis prefecture. Strictly, the applicant is a master's graduate recruited to a research job, not necessarily a PhD researcher.
