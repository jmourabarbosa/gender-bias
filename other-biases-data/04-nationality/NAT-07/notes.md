# NAT-07: The manifold costs of being a non-native English speaker in science

- **Citation:** Amano T., Ramírez-Castañeda V., Berdejo-Espinola V., Borokini I., Chowdhury S., Golivets M., González-Trujillo J. D., Montaño-Centellas F., Paudel K., White R. L., Veríssimo D. (2023). *PLOS Biology* 21(7): e3002184. DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.3002184. PMC10353817.
- **url_used:** PMC open-data bucket https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10353817.1/ (first pass: source.pdf, article.xml, figure JPGs, 35 supplementary files). Also page.html (EurekAlert press release) and page1.html (PLOS landing page).
- **Access status:** downloaded (CC BY)
- **Headline numbers found:**
  - Non-native speakers from low-English-proficiency nationalities spend a median 90.8% more time reading an English paper (moderate: 46.6%). Text p.4, Fig 1A.
  - Paper rejected because of English writing: 38.1% (moderate) and 35.9% (low) vs 14.4% (native), i.e. 2.5-2.6 times higher. Text pp.4-5, Fig 2A.
  - Often or always asked to improve English during revision: 42.5% / 42.6% vs 3.4%, i.e. 12.5 times higher. Text p.5, Fig 2B.
  - Presentation preparation: +93.7% (moderate) / +38.0% (low). Text p.6, Fig 4A.
- **Key number check (up to 90.8% more reading time; 2.5x language rejections):** MATCH
- **Data note:** the authors' ethics approval prevents sharing the raw survey data. supplementary/pbio.3002184.s027.xlsx holds supporting tables.
