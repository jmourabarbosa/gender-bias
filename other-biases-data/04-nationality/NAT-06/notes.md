# NAT-06: Campus France, Chiffres clés 2025 (press release)

- **Citation:** Campus France (2025). "Mobilité étudiante : la France consolide son attractivité dans un contexte de forte concurrence". Communiqué de presse, 20 May 2025, presenting the *Chiffres clés 2025* of student mobility. 6 pages with annexes (MESR-SIES and UNESCO/OECD data).
- **url_used:** https://www.campusfrance.org/system/files/medias/documents/2025-05/CP_Chiffres_cles_2025_FR_vdef.pdf (first pass)
- **Access status:** downloaded. This is the press release, not the full *Chiffres clés* brochure.
- **Headline number found:** "Avec plus de 430 000 étudiants étrangers accueillis en 2023-2024" (PDF p.2). Origins: North Africa-Middle East 28%, Europe 25%, sub-Saharan Africa 25%, Asia-Oceania 13%, Americas 8% (p.2; Annexe 2, p.4). Top senders: Morocco (10%), Algeria (8%), China (6%), Italy (5%), Senegal (4%) (Annexe 4, p.5).
- **Key number check (>430,000 foreign students):** MATCH with the text on p.2. Note that the annex tables (SIES) total **419,694** foreign-national students in 2023-24 (Annexes 2-4). The 430,000+ in the text presumably uses a wider scope. Say "about 420,000-430,000" or cite the scope used.
