# ETH-01: Statistiques ethniques (Insee, legal framework)

- **Citation:** Insee, "Statistiques ethniques", information page, https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/2108548 (consulted 2026-10-29). Legal basis cited on the page: Conseil constitutionnel décision n°2007-557 DC of 15 November 2007, RGPD, loi Informatique et Libertés (1978), loi de 1951 on statistical secrecy.
- **url_used:** https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/2108548 (first pass, page.html checked: genuine Insee page, not a challenge page)
- **Access status:** downloaded (web page)
- **Headline content found (page.html):**
  - The Conseil constitutionnel (15 Nov 2007) bars processing data that show racial or ethnic origins directly or indirectly, adding race or religion variables to administrative files, and defining an *a priori* "ethno-racial referential".
  - Public statistics may study diversity, discrimination and integration using **objective** data (name, geographic origin, nationality, parents' country of birth) and **subjective** data ("ressenti d'appartenance"), subject to a Cnis opinion and Cnil declaration or authorisation.
  - Example: the Insee-Ined TeO survey (2009; TeO2 in 2019-20 with 26,500 people).
- **Key number check ("Ethnic categories not allowed in public statistics"):** MATCH with a nuance. Predefined ethno-racial categories and race variables in administrative files are barred. Surveys on origin (country of birth, nationality, parents' origin) and self-reported belonging are allowed. Better wording for a slide: "no ethno-racial categories in French official statistics; origin is measured via country of birth/nationality of the person and their parents".
- **Figures:** none (text slide).
- **Implication for the talk:** no French data exist on the ethnic composition of academic staff (see OTHER_SOURCES.md "Known gaps"). ETH-04/05 (ACADISCRI) use migration link and self-/other-assigned racialised categories within a research survey.
