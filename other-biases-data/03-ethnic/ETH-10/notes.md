# ETH-10: NIH peer review: criterion scores completely account for racial disparities in overall impact scores

- **Citation:** Erosheva E. A., Grant S., Chen M.-C., Lindner M. D., Nakamura R. K., Lee C. J. (2020). *Science Advances* 6(23): eaaz4868. DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aaz4868. PMC7269672.
- **url_used:** PMC open-data bucket https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC7269672.1/ (first pass: source.pdf, article.xml, figure JPGs, supplementary/aaz4868_SM.pdf)
- **Access status:** downloaded (CC BY-NC)
- **Headline number found:** after matching (on variables including area of science), the award rate for Black applications was 75% of that for matched white applications (11.57% vs 15.39%). Unmatched: 55% (10.2% vs 18.5%). Text, PDF p.6. Criterion scores (approach, significance, ...) fully account for the gap in preliminary overall impact scores (Table 6, p.7; Fig. 2, p.6).
- **Key number check (about 75% as successful after controls):** MATCH
