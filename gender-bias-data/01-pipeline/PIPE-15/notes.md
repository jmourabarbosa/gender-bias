- Projections 2020–2060: closing the attrition gap alone (GNA) raises Natural Sciences to only 34.4 % by 2060 (+6.4 pp); with equal-rate hiring (OA+ER) mean +9.9 pp but "none of the academic domains is projected to achieve stable gender parity under this scenario because attrition remains gendered".
