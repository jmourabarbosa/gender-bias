## Headline number
- p. 1: "Women represented 44 % of Doctoral graduates in both 2021 and 2013"; p. 2: grade A share "increased from 29 % to 30 %".
- Key number "France 44% PhDs, 30% grade A" → **MATCHES**.
- Caveat: the fiche's text says France "continues to perform below the EU-27 average" for grade A, but its own Fig. 6 shows FR 30.2 % vs EU-27 29.2 % (FR line above EU). Also, the EU-27 grade A value here (29.2 %) differs slightly from the main report (29.7 %, Fig. 6.1) because the main report includes academic staff for more countries. Internal inconsistency in source, not in our key number.
