Doctorate Recipients from U.S. Universities: 2024
NSF 26-315  |  March 13, 2026
Report
The Survey of Earned Doctorates (SED), the data source for this report, is an annual census of individuals who earn research doctoral degrees from accredited U.S. academic institutions.
View Download

Data Highlights

Explore survey data
Doctorates awarded by U.S. colleges and universities: 1958–2024
The number of research doctoral degrees awarded by U.S. institutions increased from 57,806 in 2023 to 58,131 in 2024, similar to the increase between 2022 and 2023.
View analysis
325
Total increase in the number of doctorate recipients from U.S. universities between 2023 (57,806) and 2024 (58,131)
76%
Percentage increase in the number of doctorates awarded to temporary visa holders in science and engineering fields over the past 2 decades
Doctorates awarded in S&E and non-S&E fields, by citizenship status: 2004–24
Of the 43,942 S&E doctorate recipients who reported their citizenship, U.S. citizens and permanent residents earned 62% of the S&E doctorates; temporary visa holders earned 38%.
View analysis
Doctorates awarded, by sex and citizenship: 2004–24
In 2024, women earned 48% of all doctorates from U.S. institutions and men 52%. Among U.S. citizens and permanent residents, women earned 54% and men 46% of all U.S. doctorates.
View analysis
70%
Percentage of 2024 doctorate recipients with definite commitments for employment or for postdoctoral study or training (postdoc) positions who responded to the postgraduation commitment questions
45,929
Total number of science and engineering doctorates awarded by U.S. institutions in 2024
Doctorates awarded, by sex and field: 2004–24
In the past 20 years, all of the growth in the number of doctorates earned by both men and women has been in S&E fields.
View analysis
Doctorate recipients on temporary visas, by trend broad field: 2004 and 2024
In 2024, temporary visa holders earned the majority of doctorates in computer and information sciences (61%), engineering (54%), and mathematics and statistics (51%).
View analysis
65%
Increase in the number of science and engineering doctorates awarded by U.S. institutions between 2004 and 2024
79%
Proportion of science and engineering doctorates out of all doctorates awarded in the United States in 2024
Definite commitments among doctorate recipients, by S&E trend broad field: 2004–24
Between 2023 and 2024, the proportion of doctorate recipients with definite commitments declined in all S&E broad fields.
