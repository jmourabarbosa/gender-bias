# PAY-09: How parenthood contributes to gender gaps in academia

- **Headline number (from source):** Survey of 7,764 academics: gender gaps appear in the parent group only. Mothers vs fathers: OR 0.72 (95% CI 0.63-0.82) for satisfaction with research achievements, OR 1.31 (1.19-1.45) for higher work-family conflict; non-parents show no significant gap (OR 1.05 for conflict).
