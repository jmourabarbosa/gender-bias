- Applicants: men 23,484 (57.4 %), women 14,782 (36.1 %), unknown 2,645 (6.5 %).
- Funded as PI: men 6,983 (**29.7 %**), women 4,406 (**29.8 %**); unknown 421 (15.9 %).
- Text: "in recent years men and women have had similar funding rates" (gap closed since ~2021; women lagged by 2–3 pp in 2010–2017, Fig. 9A).
