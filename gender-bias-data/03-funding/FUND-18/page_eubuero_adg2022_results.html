30/03/2023

The ERC has announced the awarding of 218 Advanced Grants under the Call 2022

On 30 March, the ERC has published the results of the Advanced Grant Call 2022. In total, 218 Advanced Grants have been awarded, with a total budget of 544 million euros.

On 30 March, the ERC has published the results of the Advanced Grant Call 2022. In total, 218 Advanced Grants have been awarded, with a total budget of 544 million euros.
 
The laureates of this grant competition will carry out their projects at universities and research centres in 20 countries in Europe, with the highest number of projects in Germany (37), the UK (35), France (32) and Spain (16). The winners come from all over the world, with 27 nationalities represented, notably Germans (36 researchers), French (32), Italians (21), British (19).
 
This call for proposals attracted nearly 1,650 applications, which were reviewed by panels of renowned researchers. The overall success rate was 13.2 percent. Female researchers account for 23 percent of all applications, their highest participation rate in Advanced Grant calls up to now.
